PSCAD model validation for new and existing US plants

Committed plants must submit PSCAD models, and many older operating plants are now being asked for new or updated EMT models. GridDMAT validates and updates PSCAD models for US BESS, solar and wind plants, from commissioning through years of operation.

Discuss your project

Typical work

  • As-built model validation against commissioning and event data
  • Model updates after inverter firmware or plant controller changes, with a full test-suite re-run
  • Building or updating a PSCAD model for an older plant that only has RMS models, from OEM models and site data
  • Support for NERC MOD-026/027 model verification where the plant qualifies
  • Responses to utility, transmission owner and ISO model review comments

Markets

ERCOT, CAISO, PJM, MISO, SPP, NYISO and ISO-NE, and utility or transmission-owner specific requirements. GridDMAT focuses on plants under about 800 MW that are committed or already operating.

Frequently asked questions

Why would an operating plant need a new PSCAD model?

Grid operators increasingly ask existing inverter-based plants for EMT models, or for updated models after firmware or controller changes, because RMS models can't show some fast control interactions.

What is MOD-026/027?

NERC reliability standards for verifying generator models. MOD-026 covers excitation and plant voltage control models, and MOD-027 covers turbine and governor or active-power control models, where the plant meets the applicability criteria.

Do you work with OEM-encrypted models?

Yes, within the terms of the OEM's licence and confidentiality agreement. Encrypted OEM blocks are integrated and tested as supplied.
