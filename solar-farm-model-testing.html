Sector

Solar farm and hybrid model testing

Utility-scale solar and hybrid PV + BESS plants combine many inverters, long collector networks and a plant controller. GridDMAT tests these models to AEMO, TNSP and US ISO requirements.

Discuss your project

Key modelling issues

  • Plant controller coordination of inverters, especially in hybrids with storage
  • Reactive power capability and voltage control across operating points
  • Source energy changes, such as irradiance steps
  • Collector network representation and aggregation

Hybrids

Hybrids add a layer of complexity: the solar, battery and plant controller behaviour have to be shown together, and DC-coupled designs behave differently from AC-coupled ones.

Frequently asked questions

Does a hybrid need one model or two?

It depends on how it is registered and connected. Where solar and storage share a connection point and plant controller, the combined behaviour must be demonstrated, so the models are tested together.
