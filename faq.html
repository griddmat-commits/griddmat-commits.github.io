FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers on PSCAD/EMT model acceptance testing, DMAT, ERCOT model quality tests and grid-forming plants.

What does GridDMAT do?

GridDMAT specialises in PSCAD/EMT model acceptance testing and validation for BESS, solar, wind, hybrid and data centre grid connections in Australia and the USA.

What is a DMAT?

A Dynamic Model Acceptance Test is AEMO's defined suite of simulation cases that a generating system's RMS and EMT models must pass before they are accepted for use in NEM connection and system studies.

What is EMT modelling and why is PSCAD used?

Electromagnetic transient (EMT) models simulate fast power-electronic behaviour that RMS models can't capture, such as control interactions and behaviour in weak grids. PSCAD/EMTDC is the EMT tool most widely required by AEMO, Australian network service providers and many US system operators.

How long does model acceptance testing take?

It depends mainly on model readiness. With a healthy model and scripted test cases, a full test suite and report typically takes a few weeks per iteration; poor initialisation or missing documentation adds time. GridDMAT runs a health check first to find issues early.

What is the difference between grid-forming and grid-following?

A grid-following inverter synchronises to the measured grid voltage and injects current. A grid-forming inverter sets its own voltage and frequency reference and behaves more like a voltage source, which changes its response to faults, phase jumps and weak grids.

Does ERCOT require PSCAD models?

Yes. ERCOT requires EMT models for inverter-based resources as part of interconnection, alongside the PSS®E models. Check the current Planning Guide and Dynamic Working Group procedures for the exact requirements that apply to your project.

Which US projects do you work on?

Mainly BESS, solar, wind and hybrid plants under about 800 MW that are committed or already operating, including older plants asked for new or updated PSCAD models.

Do you work with OEM-encrypted models?

Yes, within the terms of the OEM's licence and confidentiality agreement. Encrypted OEM blocks are integrated and tested as supplied.

Where is GridDMAT based?

Brisbane, Australia, with working hours that overlap both Australian and US time zones.
