Sector

Data centre ride-through modelling

Large data centres are mostly power electronics. During a voltage dip many loads can transfer to backup at once, which the grid sees as a sudden loss of load. Grid operators now expect large loads to show how they ride through disturbances.

Discuss your project

What GridDMAT models

  • Facility behaviour during voltage and frequency disturbances, including UPS transfer logic
  • On-site BESS and backup systems, and how they coordinate with the facility
  • Ride-through evidence against the applicable requirements

Rules to know

In ERCOT, NOGRR282 and NPRR1308 (PUCT approved 9 July 2026, effective 1 August 2026) set ride-through requirements for Large Electronic Loads: sites above 75 MW with at least half computational demand. In Australia, large-load connection rules are under active consideration by the AEMC.

Frequently asked questions

Which data centres does NOGRR282 apply to?

Large Electronic Loads in ERCOT: sites above 75 MW where at least half of demand is computational. Loads approved to energise, or with completed studies and interconnection agreements, before 14 November 2025 are exempt.
