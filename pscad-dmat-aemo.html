PSCAD DMAT for AEMO and NEM connections

GridDMAT runs the full Dynamic Model Acceptance Test (DMAT) suite in PSCAD for battery, solar, wind and hybrid plants connecting to the National Electricity Market, built to the AEMO DMAT Guideline and your network service provider's requirements.

Discuss your project

What the DMAT checks

AEMO's Dynamic Model Acceptance Test Guideline (Version 3.0, effective 3 June 2024) defines the cases a plant's RMS and EMT models must pass before AEMO and the network service provider use them in connection and system studies. The suite covers:

  • Flat run and initialisation
  • Balanced and unbalanced faults, including multiple fault ride-through
  • Temporary overvoltage
  • Voltage, reactive power, power factor and active power setpoint steps
  • Frequency ramps
  • Grid voltage changes and phase angle jumps
  • Grid oscillation rejection
  • Very low system strength (SCR = 1)
  • Source energy changes

Passing the DMAT shows the model is numerically sound, well initialised and behaves as documented. It is separate from demonstrating that the plant meets its performance standards.

How GridDMAT delivers it

  • Model intake and health check before the formal suite: initialisation, timestep and parameter consistency
  • Scripted test cases, so re-runs after a firmware or setting change take days, not weeks
  • Assessment against the guideline's criteria, with consistent plots and a submission-ready report
  • Responses to AEMO and TNSP review comments until the model is accepted
  • RMS (PSS®E) to EMT (PSCAD) benchmarking where required

Who it's for

Developers, owners, EPCs, OEMs and connection consultants delivering BESS (including grid-forming), utility-scale solar, wind and hybrid projects connecting through Transgrid, Powerlink, AusNet, ElectraNet, TasNetworks or distribution networks.

Frequently asked questions

What is a DMAT?

A Dynamic Model Acceptance Test is AEMO's defined suite of simulation cases that a generating system's RMS and EMT models must pass before they are accepted for use in NEM connection and system studies.

Which DMAT guideline version applies?

AEMO's DMAT Guideline Version 3.0, effective 3 June 2024, is the current version. Always confirm with AEMO and your network service provider which version and any NSP-specific additions apply to your application.

Does passing the DMAT mean the plant meets its performance standards?

No. The DMAT shows the model is credible and well behaved. Compliance with the negotiated performance standards is assessed separately.
