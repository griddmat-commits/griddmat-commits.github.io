Wind farm model testing

Wind farms combine many turbine generators, long collector networks and a plant controller. GridDMAT tests Type 3 (doubly-fed) and Type 4 (full-converter) wind farm models to AEMO, TNSP and US ISO requirements.

Discuss your project

Key modelling issues

  • Type 3 and Type 4 turbines respond differently to faults and weak-grid conditions
  • Multiple fault ride-through and the turbine's protection and chopper behaviour
  • Collector network representation and turbine aggregation
  • Plant controller and turbine controller interaction

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Type 3 and Type 4 wind turbines?

A Type 3 turbine uses a doubly-fed induction generator with a partially rated converter, while a Type 4 uses a fully rated converter between the generator and the grid. Their fault and weak-grid behaviour differs, which shows in EMT studies.
