ERCOT PSCAD model quality tests

Inverter-based plants connecting in ERCOT must provide EMT (PSCAD) models that pass ERCOT's model quality checks. GridDMAT builds and runs those test suites for batteries, solar, wind and hybrids, and supports large electronic loads under NOGRR282.

Discuss your project

What GridDMAT covers

  • Model package review: completeness, initialisation, numerical stability and documentation
  • Model quality test runs in PSCAD, with results assessed against ERCOT's acceptance criteria
  • Ride-through behaviour, including the IBR ride-through requirements under NOGRR245
  • PSCAD vs PSS®E benchmarking where the study scope requires it
  • Re-runs after OEM model or firmware updates, and responses to ERCOT and TSP review comments

Large electronic loads (data centres)

ERCOT's NOGRR282 and NPRR1308, approved by the PUCT on 9 July 2026 and effective 1 August 2026, set voltage and frequency ride-through requirements for Large Electronic Loads: sites above 75 MW where at least half of demand is computational. Loads approved to energise, or with completed studies and interconnection agreements, before 14 November 2025 are exempt. GridDMAT models facility, UPS and on-site BESS behaviour to show ride-through performance.

When to engage

As soon as an interconnection agreement is in place or energisation is within about 18 months. Earlier engagement leaves time to fix model issues before they reach the critical path.

Frequently asked questions

Does ERCOT require PSCAD models?

Yes. ERCOT requires EMT models for inverter-based resources as part of interconnection, alongside the PSS®E models. Check the current Planning Guide and Dynamic Working Group procedures for the exact requirements that apply to your project.

What is NOGRR282?

An ERCOT Nodal Operating Guide revision setting voltage and frequency ride-through requirements for Large Electronic Loads, such as data centres above 75 MW with mostly computational demand. The PUCT approved it on 9 July 2026, effective 1 August 2026.

Can you re-run tests after an OEM firmware update?

Yes. GridDMAT's test cases are scripted, so a full re-run after a model or firmware change is fast and repeatable.
